brand-skills.md — DIESEL: THE ESSENCE OF DENIM

1. CAMPAIGN CORE IDENTITY

Brand: DIESEL (Fall 2026)
Campaign Title: The Essence of Denim — For Successful Living
Core Narrative: Unfiltered, unapologetic 1990s New York City street realism meets eccentric surrealism. Real people caught off-guard in the gritty heartbeat of Manhattan and Brooklyn, clad in timeless Diesel raw indigo and pumice stonewashed denim with signature waistband button hardware.

WHAT IS ON-BRAND
  • 1990s Film Aesthetic: 35mm point-and-shoot camera quality, intense direct flash, rich film grain (Kodak Portra 800 / Fuji Superia).
  • Unstaged & Candid: Subjects looking away, unaware of the lens, mid-bite, mid-yell, sleeping, or caught mid-stride.
  • Saturated Jewel Tones: Deep indigo denim against yellow cabs, neon signs, vibrant red winter coats, bright beanies.
  • NYC Street Textures: Dirty subway tiles, steaming manholes, yellow curbs, brick brownstones, bodega storefronts.
  • Absurdity & Oddity: Bizarre pets (goose, iguana, pigeon), weird props (boombox, upside down paper), unexpected posture.
WHAT IS OFF-BRAND (FORBIDDEN)
  • Clean Studio Backdrops: Seamless white cycloramas or sanitized modern fashion studios.
  • Posing & Smiling: Cheerful smile-at-camera Instagram influencer postures or runway poses.
  • Modern Digital Look: Ultra-smooth HDR, plastic AI skin, bokeh blur, sterile modern LED lighting.
  • Non-NYC Settings: European castles, generic modern minimalist apartments, beaches, or futuristic cities.
  • Generic Athleisure: Sweatpants, modern spandex, or synthetic activewear without prominent denim.

2. PHOTOGRAPHY & LIGHTING SPECIFICATIONS

Images must evoke 1990s counter-culture magazines like The Face, Dazed & Confused, and early Terry Richardson / Juergen Teller / Nan Goldin street reportage. Hard direct ring-flash or on-camera speedlite creating stark shadows behind subjects; deep rich saturation in primary colors; heavy natural analog film grain.

3. KEY ARCHIVE GARMENTS

• D-STRUKT: Slim-straight rigid raw dark indigo denim with distinct coin-pocket diagonal stitch.
• D-WENT: Relaxed 90s bootcut stonewash denim with heavy leg whiskering and frayed hems.
• Iconic Waistband: Prominent metal tack button fly with "DIESEL FOR SUCCESSFUL LIVING" branding.
